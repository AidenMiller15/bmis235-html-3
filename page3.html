Zhangjiajie National Forest Park

The Zhangjiajie National Forest Park is located in South Central China and is home to some of the most unique lanscapes in the world. The National park is famous for its thousands of towering quartz-sandstone pillars that look like floating mountains in the mist. Many people comapare the monument to the movie Avatar because its climate supports hundreds of species of woody plants and rare trees, making for an asethetic that doesn't look real.
